The one constraint that explains every other decision

Contrast the reader's existing mental model against the real one. The shape that works: "X looks like a CORRECTNESS problem. It is actually a CONSTRAINT problem wearing a correctness costume."

Then state the constraint as a number, and immediately state what a human feels when it is missed. The number alone is inert; the consequence is what the reader remembers and what stops the constraint being traded away.

Why this is not THE THING THEY FEAR
